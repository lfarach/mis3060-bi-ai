**Reflection** (2–3 sentences)

I used Claude Cowork inside my Wildcat Capital project to explore business context questions about credit risk and portfolio management, and to compare three different prompting strategies (zero-shot, role-assigned, few-shot) on the same question about credit risk metrics. What surprised me was how much the role-assigned prompt changed the usefulness of the answer without changing the underlying facts — just giving Claude an audience made the response far more decision-oriented instead of just a list of definitions. I had to fact-check a specific numeric claim (the 43% DTI threshold) against the CFPB's actual rules and found that Claude presented an outdated regulatory detail as if it were still current, which taught me not to trust specific numbers or thresholds from Claude without verifying them against a primary source first. Next time, I'd verify claims like this as I go instead of waiting until the end, since it's easy to build an answer around a "fact" that turns out to be stale.
